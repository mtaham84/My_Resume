About Me

I'm Taha Mousavi, a Computer Engineering student at the Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST), with a strong foundation in software development, algorithms, and problem-solving. I enjoy turning ideas into working software. My experience ranges from building real-world websites and backend systems with Django to developing algorithmic and machine learning projects using Python and C++. I have also worked on projects for real businesses, including designing and developing a digital presence for a cosmetics and skincare company. My current focus is moving toward Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and intelligent systems. I am particularly interested in understanding how data, algorithms, and software engineering can come together to solve practical problems. Alongside my technical work, I have served as a Teaching Assistant for Python Programming at IUST, helping students understand programming concepts, debug their code, and approach problems more effectively. I am a self-driven learner who enjoys going beyond tutorials and building things from scratch. Whether I am working with algorithms, backend systems, Linux environments, data, or machine learning models, I am motivated by the same goal: understanding how things work and using that knowledge to build something useful.
